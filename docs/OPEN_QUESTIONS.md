# Open questions

Research gaps that would change a funding decision are maintained in `research/data/evidence-gaps.csv` (G1–G20). They are published at `/evidence/gaps.html` ("Where the evidence is thin").

## Editorial and research questions still open

1. **Measures not yet assessed.** Is there sourced evidence on tree planting as a citywide PM2.5 measure, on indoor plants, on dashboards that trigger no action, and on one-day bans? None is listed as assessed yet.
2. **Missing source studies.** What do current source-apportionment studies show for Bangkok, Hanoi and Metro Manila?
3. **Project results.** What do the World Bank results frameworks for the Bangladesh, Nepal, Punjab, Uttar Pradesh and Haryana projects commit to measure, and when?
4. **Funders not yet checked.** Have the funders named in the brief but not yet checked made specific clean-air grants in South or Southeast Asia?
5. **Overlapping sensor networks.** Do the IIT Kanpur AMRIT and ATMAN networks overlap, and who funds each?
6. **Ambiguous philanthropic total.** Is the US$478 million in Clean Air Fund's 2026 philanthropic report a single-year or a five-year figure?
7. **Cambodia.** What are the budget and timeline of the CCAC-funded Cambodia rice-straw project?
8. **Philippines.** What are the funding structure and budget of E-Mobility ASAP?
9. **Unread research.** What does the CHI 2026 paper on marginalised livelihoods actually find?

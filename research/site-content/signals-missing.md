Some of the groups and questions this site cares about most seldom reach news feeds or research indexes in a form these rules can pick up. The counts below show how many records currently held touch each area. A low count is a reason to look harder, not evidence that nothing exists; a research gap is recorded only after the separate review described on the [evidence gaps page](../evidence/gaps.html).

Areas that the evidence edition already identifies as weakly documented:

- **Workers' incomes and health.** Street vendors, waste pickers, construction and transport workers, and the earnings they lose on high-pollution days.
- **Community-level exposure.** Measurements in informal settlements and on the urban and rural fringe, where monitors are scarce. Being unmonitored does not make a settlement low-exposure.
- **School indoor air.** Classroom concentrations and what protection achieves.
- **Waste and open burning.** The people who work in it and live beside it.
- **Cross-border airsheds.** Pollution that crosses state and national lines, and the institutions that share responsibility for it.

Feeds in local languages, state agencies and many community organisations are not yet connected. The [sources page](sources.html) lists what is and is not covered.

This page separates four things that are often confused: search indexing, use as input by AI tools, use for training AI models, and reuse of the content itself.

## Search indexing

Search engines may crawl and index every public page. The [robots.txt](../robots.txt) file allows all crawlers and points to the sitemap. Robots.txt is a convention that well-behaved crawlers follow. It is not a licence and does not control what anyone does with content once read.

## AI tools reading the site to answer a question

AI assistants may read the site, including the [llms.txt](../llms.txt) index, the Markdown copies of each page and the evidence packs, to answer a user's question. The [page for AI assistants](../for-ai/index.html) explains the scope and limits. Attribution with the page link and claim ID is requested.

## AI model training

The owner has not yet decided whether the site's original writing may be used to train AI models. Until that decision is recorded here, no permission for training is granted or implied by this page or by robots.txt.

## Reusing the content

The licence for Clean Air's original writing, data tables and graphics has not yet been decided by the owner. Until it is, quoting short passages with attribution and a link is welcome, and the data downloads carry a request to attribute and to check each record's sources and date.

Third-party material keeps its own rights. Source titles, quotations, reports, court documents and any external figures belong to their authors and publishers. Nothing on this site relicenses them.

## What this site does not offer

There is no API, no account, no rate-limited endpoint and no authentication. Everything is a static file served by GitHub Pages.

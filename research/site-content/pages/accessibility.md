---
title: "Accessibility"
summary: "Accessibility target, checks run, and known limits."
---

The target is WCAG 2.2 AA. Every release is checked automatically with axe for WCAG 2.1 A and AA rules, plus keyboard focus, layout at 320 pixels wide, 200% text size and print.

**Known limits.** No manual screen-reader test has yet been done. Some evidence tables are wide and scroll sideways inside their frame on small screens. Charts are avoided where a table or sentence works better.

To report a barrier, use the "Correction" issue template and mention accessibility.

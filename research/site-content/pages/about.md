---
title: "About Clean Air"
summary: "An independent evidence resource on air pollution, livelihoods and unequal protection."
---

Clean Air is an independent, evidence-led public resource. It explains how air pollution affects people in India and across South and Southeast Asia, and what could realistically improve their situation.

**Independence.** Clean Air is not a fundraising campaign and does not rank organisations. Mention of an organisation, funder or programme does not imply its endorsement. Clean Air is not affiliated with Practical Action or any organisation named in the research.

**Method.** Every factual statement links to a claim, and every claim links to its sources with publication and access dates. See [how evidence is handled](../evidence/index.html).

**Conflicts of interest.** The research was produced for a foundation audience considering grantmaking. No organisation reviewed or paid for its content. Findings that cut against funding, such as the "not recommended" list, are published alongside the rest.

**Research edition.** Evidence is as of 22 November 2026. Later evidence is not mixed in. See the [update policy](update-policy.html).

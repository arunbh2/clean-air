---
title: "Update policy"
summary: "How the evidence edition is dated and refreshed."
---

This is the **22 November 2026 evidence edition**. Nothing published after that date is included.

A future update will be a new dated edition with its own cutoff. The previous edition's files will be preserved, and the change of cutoff will be recorded in the repository's project rules. Each country dossier shows its own last-reviewed date, and the [coverage dashboard](../evidence/coverage.html) flags stale or provisional records.

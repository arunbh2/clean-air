---
title: "Corrections"
summary: "How to report an error and how corrections are recorded."
---

If you find a factual error, a broken source or a misread study, please open an issue in the public repository using the "Correction" template. Include the claim ID (for example C016) or the page address, the source you believe is correct, and its date.

Corrections are checked against the original source, and the claim register is updated with a note. Every change is visible in the repository history. Phase 2 corrections are listed in the [verification record](../evidence/verification.html).

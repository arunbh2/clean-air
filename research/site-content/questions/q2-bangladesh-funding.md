The largest verified record is public development finance. The World Bank approved the Bangladesh Clean Air Project on 18 June 2025 [C120]. The amount recorded is {{amount:P01}}. The source describes this as IDA financing and, in the release read, does not say whether it is a credit or a grant. The recorded stage is *{{approval:P01}}*; the operational stage is *{{operational:P01}}*. An approval is not a disbursement, and no source read reports how much has been spent.

Smaller grants support monitoring by Bangladeshi organisations. The EPIC Air Quality Fund awarded grants to Smart Air Bangladesh, for monitors around Dhaka's landfills [C179], and to OBHIZATRIK Foundation, for sensors in informal settlements and industrial zones [C180]. The amounts of these awards are not published in the sources read. The University of Dhaka is named in a Clean Air Fund black-carbon research grant made with Stockholm University [C170].

What this does not show: whether the approved programme has delivered its planned outputs, or whether any of this funding has improved air quality. Those questions are open in the evidence gaps listed below.

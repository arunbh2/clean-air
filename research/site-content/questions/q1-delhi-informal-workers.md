The verified records name a small group of organisations working where air pollution meets informal work in Delhi.

- **Measuring harm.** Chintan, a non-governmental organisation, measured lung function among waste pickers, sanitation workers and security guards and compared them with office workers [C082].
- **Organising and informing.** Mahila Housing Trust and Help Delhi Breathe train women from unrecognised settlements as air quality ambassadors who carry portable monitors [C195].
- **Income when work stops.** Construction workers' unions went to court over compensation when the emergency plan halts building work, and the Supreme Court directed that affected workers are to be compensated [C039]. Nationally, 44% of the construction workers' welfare cess collected up to November 2022 was unspent, according to a reply in Parliament [C101].
- **Entitlements.** Municipal ID cards for waste pickers were required by the 2016 rules, but most urban local bodies had not issued them, according to the waste pickers' federation [C107].

What this does not show: none of these records includes an independent evaluation showing reduced exposure or better health. Activity is documented; impact is not yet measured. Inclusion here is not an endorsement.

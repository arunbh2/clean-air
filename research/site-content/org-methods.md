## Who is included

An organisation is included when a public source, dated on or before 22 November 2026, documents a specific air-pollution activity it carried out, funded, regulated or evaluated. A broad environmental remit is not enough. Being listed at an event or in a network is not enough. Name similarity is never treated as a relationship.

Every organisation has one primary type and may have several roles. Every link between an organisation and a project has an exact role: grantmaker, lender, implementer, delivery partner, technical partner, supplier, evaluator, regulator, public duty holder, operator or network member.

## Which sources count for what

1. Government, regulator and multilateral records establish approvals, amounts and official roles.
2. Funders' grant databases and awardee pages establish that a grant was made and what it was for. They do not establish results.
3. Peer-reviewed studies and independent evaluations, such as sensor co-location tests, establish performance.
4. An organisation's own reports establish what it says it did. Figures from them are labelled "organisation-reported".
5. Company pages establish what a company sells and claims. They are labelled "company-reported".
6. Journalism is used for court rulings, events and scrutiny, and is labelled as journalism.
7. Search-engine snippets are never used for a published fact. Leads seen only in snippets are in the candidate log.

## Activity, output, outcome

Each project separates what was done (activities), what was produced (outputs), what was measured to change (outcomes) and whether anyone independent checked. Most records stop at activities and outputs. "No adequate evidence found" is shown where no outcome is documented. A sensor's tested accuracy is not evidence of cleaner air. A law passed by one chamber is not a law in force. A trained participant is not an avoided illness.

## Companies

Companies are included as solution providers, as operators adopting cleaner practice, or as emitters facing rules. Their records state what they sell, whether an independent test exists, and any conflict of interest. The words "proven", "effective", "best" and "impactful" are not used for any company, and the validator rejects them. Philanthropy's useful role with companies is usually independent testing, shared data infrastructure and safeguards for workers and small firms, not subsidy of a product.

## Money

Amounts appear only where a primary record states them, with currency, year and stage (approved, awarded, disbursed). A loan is never added to a grant. "We could not find a public grant record" does not mean "no one funds this".

## Cutoff and freshness

Evidence is searched up to 22 November 2026. Pages were read on 22 or 23 November 2026. Each record shows when it was last checked. Projects whose funding after 2025 could not be confirmed, for example those funded by USAID, are labelled historical rather than active.

## What this directory is not

It is not a ranking, an endorsement or a list of organisations to fund. No organisation was contacted. No personal contact details are published. Logos are not used.

## Corrections

To report an error or a missing organisation, open an issue in the public repository using the correction or new-source template. Every change is made in the research files and the site is regenerated.

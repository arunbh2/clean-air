---
title: Blind spots
summary: Known limits of the feed's coverage.
---

**Known blind spots.** Government agencies in India (CPCB, CAQM, MoEFCC and the Press Information Bureau) publish no working feed that accepts automated requests, so official notices reach this page only through news coverage or a manual record. Most feeds are in English; Hindi, Bengali, Nepali, Urdu, Thai, Vietnamese, Bahasa and other local-language reporting is largely missing. Sri Lanka, Bhutan, Cambodia, Laos and Myanmar have no direct feed. Two World Bank interfaces responded but returned no current records, so World Bank material appears only through other sources. Newsroom feeds list only their latest items, so a story published and dropped between two refreshes can be missed.

This map connects the sources of air pollution to what happens in the air, where people breathe it, what it does to health, earnings and families, and the institutions and money that could change it.

**How to read it.** Boxes are parts of the system. Arrows show a documented or hypothesised connection. A solid thick line is a direct effect (from a source or from the air); a solid thin line is a knock-on effect; a dotted line is a reasoned hypothesis that has not been measured. Select any box or arrow to see the evidence. The same content is listed on the page [every link as text](links.html).

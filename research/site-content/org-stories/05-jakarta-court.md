## The problem in everyday language

Jakarta residents asked a simple question about their polluted air: who is legally responsible?

## The actors and their different roles

Koalisi Ibukota, a citizens' coalition, brought the lawsuit [C209]. Greenpeace Indonesia publicised it and campaigns for the ruling to be carried out [C208] [C209]. Bicara Udara, a separate non-profit, campaigns for clean-air policy through ambassadors, talks and schools [C203].

## What they did

On 16 September 2021 the Central Jakarta District Court found seven officials negligent, including the President and three governors [C208]. The Supreme Court rejected the government's final appeal on 13 November 2023 [C209].

## Who paid

Litigation costs are not documented in the sources read.

## What evidence of change exists

A legal outcome is documented. The coalition said after the final appeal that the government still had to act on the ruling [C209]. No source read measures compliance or air-quality change.

## What remains uncertain

Which orders have been carried out, by whom, and with what effect on emissions.

## What another place could learn

Courts can establish responsibility where laws exist. Turning rulings into action needs monitoring of compliance, which the courts themselves rarely provide.

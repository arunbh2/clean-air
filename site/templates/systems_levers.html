Where action could weaken a link

In short

Each lever names the connection on the map that it could weaken. Its rating says, separately, how strong the evidence is that the action improves outcomes. Cutting pollution at its source is different from protecting people while sources persist, and both differ from supporting health and income.

A lever could weaken a connection; it does not guarantee an outcome. Back to the map.

Reading these records

  • Approval, operation and evidence are three separate stages. A programme can be approved with no results yet.
  • Amounts appear only where a primary record states them. The amount type says whether it is approved, disbursed, or a project total.
  • Philanthropic grant amounts are often not published. Where they are missing, the record says so.
  • Global totals from different reports use different periods and bases, and are never added together {{ "C141;C142"|claims(rel)|safe }}.

What will it take to deliver this?

How does Lahore compare with Delhi?

Both sit in the same Indo-Gangetic airshed, and smoke crosses the border between them {{ "C125;C149"|claims(rel)|safe }}. But their source numbers come from different kinds of study. Lahore's comes from an emissions inventory (what is released), and Delhi's from measurements at monitors (what arrives) {{ "C149;C025"|claims(rel)|safe }}. They cannot be compared directly.

  • What may transfer: crop-residue alternatives, worker compensation during restrictions, and airshed-wide planning {{ "C067;C039;C125"|claims(rel)|safe }}.
  • Caveats: different monitoring networks, different AQI formulas, and inventories that miss informal sources {{ "C066;C150"|claims(rel)|safe }}.

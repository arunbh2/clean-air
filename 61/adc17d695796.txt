 Grants

 TRUE Initiative in India 
 
 ICCT 
 
 
 
 
 To assess the real-world emissions of the vehicle fleet in Delhi, India, to inform policy actions that reduce transport emissions and help the city to meet its clean air action plan goals. 
 
 Data India 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 

                        2022
                       
 
 
 
 Breathe Providence 
 
 Brown University 
 
 
 
 
 To understand and raise awareness of air pollution and climate emissions in Providence, advance use of lower-cost air quality monitoring data, support improved policy and environmental initiatives and enable Brown University to support the City of Providence’s health, wellness and climate related goals. 
 
 Data Cities 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 

                        2022
                       
 
 
 
 Improving global PM2.5 data 
 
 SPARTAN 
 
 
 
 
 To support a global network of advanced PM2.5 monitoring sites that improves the quality of global air quality data resources (e.g.  global health impact assessments) by improving satellite data and global-scale air quality models. 
 
 Data Global 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 

                        2021
                       
 
 
 
 Integrated online air pollution monitoring and decision support system 
 
 IIT-Kanpur 
 
 
 
 
 To develop an Artificial Intelligence (AI) based Decision Support System (DSS) for Lucknow (UP) to integrate air quality from various sources data at one point. 
 
 Society India 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 

                        2021
                       
 
 
 
 Clean air community mobilisation 
 
 British Lung Foundation 
 
 
 
 
 To build an even larger network of air quality campaigners across the UK to put pressure on local authorities to deliver clean air to their constituents. This project will maintain and grow campaigning activities in Manchester, Liverpool and Birmingham, as well as West Yorkshire and Glasgow. 
 
 Society United Kingdom 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 

                        2021
                       
 
 
 
 Supporting LeadAir city leadership programme 
 
 Forum Energii 
 
 
 
 
 Six Polish cities will prepare plans to 100% comply with EU CAFE and NEC Directives and showcase to the national government it is possible to achieve ambitious AQ standards. 
 
 Society European Union 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 

                        2021
                       
 
 
 
 Encouraging effective implementation of antismog laws in regions 
 
 Association Krakow Smog Alert 
 
 
 
 
 Through campaigning and advocacy activities, enforcement of anti-smog resolutions will be strengthened in 3 Polish regions. 
 
 Society European Union 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 

                        2021
                       
 
 
 
 Open AQ 2021 – 2022 
 
 OpenAQ 
 
 
 
 
 To support OpenAQ and its work to aggregate and make available the world’s air quality data. 
 
 Data Global 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 

                        2021
                       
 
 
 
 Air Sensors International Conference: International Connection Hub 
 
 UC Davis 
 
 
 
 
 Bring the relevant and latest knowledge on air quality data (including but not limited to low-cost monitoring, data analysis, source characterisation and air quality management) to the stakeholders in low- and middle-income countries which is currently poorly disseminated from Global North contexts. 
 
 Data Global 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 

                        2021
                       
 
 
 
 Air quality data policy tools 
 
 World Resources Institute 
 
 
 
 
 To help stakeholders in low- and middle- income countries access the latest knowledge and expertise on air quality data (including but not limited to low-cost monitoring, data analysis, source characterisation and air quality management) which is currently poorly disseminated from Global North contexts.

 Health professionals in action on air pollution in Bulgaria in 2023 
 
 Air for Health 
 
 
 
 
 To raise awareness of health impacts of air pollution among doctors to become powerful spokespeople raising the awareness and providing solutions to patients, the public and policy makers. This project will create a medical student network to support them as they go onto careers.

                        2022
                       
 
 
 
 Enabling universal access to air quality data to support clean air action 
 
 OpenAQ 
 
 
 
 
 Enabling OpenAQ's platform to serve as the most useful and accessible air quality data platform in the world due to such qualities as being freely available; providing the greatest geographical coverage;  providing centralised and directly comparable data; providing  real-time data and historical data not typically saved on other platforms; and providing tools that allow stakeholders with varying  technical competencies to use the OpenAQ platform to produce the evidence needed to demand clean air action.

 Developing and deploying Low Emissions Zones in Poland 
 
 Polskie Stowarzyszenie Paliw Alternatywnych 
 
 
 
 
 This project aims to build guidance and establish a process for developing low emission zones in Poland and to create an enabling environment at the local level, for example through the provision of expert support to local officials and decision-makers throughout LEZ development and implementation. 
 
 Cities European Union 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 

                        2022
                       
 
 
 
 Clean air co-benefits in the EU 
 
 European Policy Centre 
 
 
 
 
 The project aims to demonstrate the economic and social benefits of cleaner air and showcase the urgency for action. It will build on existing data to show the linkages between air pollution and key policy areas, map and analyse the existing policy framework, including the revision of the Ambient Air Quality Directive, and put forward policy recommendations for further action. 
 
 Climate Economy European Union

 Air pollution in the 2023 local elections 
 
 Polityka Insight 
 
 
 
 
 To put air quality on the political agenda of the local elections through key mayoral candidates and to make the public case more about their candidate's actions regarding air pollution when choosing who to vote for.

 Making air quality policies more inclusive and equitable 
 
 Chintan 
 
 
 
 
 Build widespread acknowledgement of the impact of air pollution on categories of essential workers whose work exposes them to such vulnerability, ensuring that solutions to air pollution are not only technically sound but also equitable and inclusive.

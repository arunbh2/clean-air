 Delhi govt to install 400 additional tents to shelter more of the capital’s homeless during winter

  New Delhi, Nov 16 (PTI) The Delhi government will install around 400 additional waterproof and fire-resistent tents to accommodate another 4,000 of the capital’s destitutes this winter, taking the total capacity of homeless shelters in Delhi to more than 11,000, according to the Delhi Urban Shelter Improvement Board’s (DUSIB) Winter Action Plan 2026-27. 
 
 
  DUSIB currently operates around 197 shelter homes across Delhi with a combined capacity of 7,092 people. Of these, 82 shelters are located in permanent reinforced cement concrete (RCC) buildings, while 115 operate from porta-cabin structures. 
 
 
 
  The additional 400 pagoda tents will be installed at locations with a high concentration of homeless people to meet the increased demand for shelter during winter. More tents will also be set up if required, the plan states.

  The winter action plan was operationalised Sunday, November 15, and will remain in force till March 15, 2027, to shelter Delhi’s homeless population from the severe cold. 
  As part of the arrangements, 15 rescue teams formed by shelter management agencies and NGOs will operate daily between 10 pm and 4 am to bring homeless people sleeping on roadsides, in open spaces and parks to nearby shelter homes or hospitals, depending on requirements. 
  Each rescue team will comprise a vehicle, a driver and two attendants and will operate within the jurisdiction of the respective shelter management agency, the plan says, adding that the teams will respond to information received from the DUSIB control room and other sources. 
  As per the plan, a centralised control room with dedicated staff is already functional at Punarwas Bhawan in I P Estate to monitor rescue operations. It will receive complaints and information round-the-clock through a dedicated helpline, mobile application and other sources and alert rescue teams. 
  A Joint Apex Advisory Committee (JAAC), headed by the DUSIB chief executive officer, will coordinate with representatives of shelter management agencies, NGOs and nodal officers of the Delhi Jal Board, health department, municipal corporations and police to ensure the availability of civic amenities at the shelters, the plan reads. 
  Representatives of AIIMS, Safdarjung Hospital, LNJP Hospital and Lady Hardinge Medical College have also been included in a committee to facilitate coordination on health-related matters, the plan mentions. 
  The shelter homes provide rugs, mattresses, bedsheets, pillows, blankets, drinking water, toilets, electricity, lighting, television, first-aid boxes and lockers for belongings wherever available. Bathing facilities are also available at shelters wherever provided, while hot water is made available during winter wherever the facility exists, the plan says. 
  Caretakers will remain available in every shelter home in three eight-hour shifts. Women security guards will also be deployed in every shift at women’s shelter homes. PTI SHB ARB
